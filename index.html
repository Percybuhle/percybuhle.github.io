My journey into digital marketing has been driven by curiosity, creativity, and a passion for helping organizations communicate their stories effectively. After earning my Bachelor of Science in Development Studies in Zimbabwe, I moved to New York to pursue a Master of Science in Digital Marketing & Media at Yeshiva University.

During my graduate studies, I gained hands-on experience across digital strategy, CRM, marketing analytics, consumer behavior, branding, and social media marketing. Beyond the classroom, I've volunteered as a Graduate Teaching Assistant and served as Digital Media & Community Manager for the African Diaspora Youth Hub — experiences that sharpened my leadership, communication, project management, and content creation skills while letting me contribute to nonprofit initiatives that matter to me.

I believe great marketing is built on understanding people, telling authentic stories, and using data to make informed decisions. My goal is to help organizations grow through thoughtful digital marketing strategies that create lasting impact.
